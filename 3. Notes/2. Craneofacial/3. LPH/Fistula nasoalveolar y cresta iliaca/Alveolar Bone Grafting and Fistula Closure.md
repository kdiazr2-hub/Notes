> [!info] Introduction
> Nasoalveolar fistula are remanent from cleft lip-palate. There is controversy about when to do the closure of the fistula, Nevertheless, It has been proved that this surgery enhance- ment of orthodontic and prosthodontic treatment, stabi- lization of the maxillary arch, and improvement in oral hygiene through the elimination of oronasal fistulae. 
>

> [!important] Operative technique: 
> 
> 1. Placement of the Digman for better visualization. 
> 
> 2. Keyhole incision around the fistula.
> 
> 3. Buccal flaps on either side of the cleft are raised in a subperiosteal fashion using incisions made bilaterally along the buccal/labial dento-gingivo junction that are continuous with the key-hole fistula incisions. 
> 
> 4. Bilateral relaxing incisions aimed vertically near the first molars are made followed by subperiosteal exposure of the anterior maxilla. 
> 
> 5. The underlying periosteum of the raised buccal flaps is then scored to facilitate later flap mobilization.
> 
> 6. If the fistula includes a palatal component, the keyhole incision is extended circumferentially along the palatal fistula. 
> 
> 7. Palatal flaps are then raised bilaterally along the lin- gual dento-gingivo junction and raised in a subperiosteal fashion.
> 
> 8. At the fistula defect, careful dissection of both the buccal and palatal flaps from each other is performed, followed by dissection of the nasal floor from these two dissected buccal and palatal layers. 
> 
> 9. Extensive subperios- teal dissection along the piriform aperture and maxillary crest is performed to achieve dissection beyond the cleft defect and fistula in all three layers.
> 
> 10. The nasal soft tissue is pushed into the nasal cavity and closed in a water tight fashion. 
> 
> 11. After closure of the nasal side, a saline syringe is inserted into one of the nasal passages and egress of saline fluids is evaluated at the nasal closure.
> 
> 12. Cancellous bone from the iliac crest is harvested.
> 
> 13. The mucoperiosteal buccal and palatal flaps are advanced and closed over the surface of the former fistula/alveolar defect.
