Brent Technique:

This technique focuses on multi-stage reconstruction to achieve the best possible detail.

The first stage involves harvesting cartilage, creating the framework, and inserting it into the subcutaneous pocket in the skin of the auricular region.

The second stage involves transposing the earlobe.

The third stage is the separation of the ear from the skull and the placement of a skin graft to form the auriculocephalic sulcus.

The fourth stage consists of constructing the tragus and performing otoplasty on the contralateral side.
